Choose Screen Color

This dialog lets you choose the screen color for AbiWord. The easiest way to do this is to click on the color ring at the left of the dialog to choose the color, and on the triangle to pick how dark or light it is. The rectangle under the wheel shows the new color in its right half.

You can also change the color by changing the values in the boxes to the right of the color ring, or by typing the hexadecimal code for the color in the box. This is useful when you know exactly what color you want to match.

This dialog only affects the display. It does not change the way that the document is printed.
